# Paris Travel Guide

## Eiffel Tower
The Eiffel Tower is a wrought-iron lattice tower on the Champ de Mars in Paris, France. It is named after the engineer Gustave Eiffel, whose company designed and built the tower.

## Louvre Museum
The Louvre, or the Louvre Museum, is the world's most-visited museum and a historic monument in Paris, France. It is the home of the Mona Lisa.

## Cuisine
French cuisine consists of the cooking traditions and practices from France. Cheese and wine are a major part of the cuisine.
